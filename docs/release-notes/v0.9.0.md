# Scroblytics 0.9.0 (beta)

The first public release of Scroblytics, a native Mac app for exploring and managing your [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm) listening
history. Your history is copied to your Mac and stays there. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

This is a **beta**: it is usable every day, but it is young. Please report anything that looks wrong.

## What's in it

- **Overview, Top Charts and Library.** Your most-played artists, albums and tracks for any date range, with how each moved against the
  previous period, plus a full library view with artwork, tags, track lengths, tempos and release dates.
- **Trends, Timeline and Milestones.** Listening over time, streaks, heatmaps, "on this day", round-number milestones, and how your taste
  has changed.
- **Ask.** Type a request such as "my most played album from each release year" and see how it was understood, as filters you can
  adjust, before anything runs. Use Apple's on-device model, or a larger model with your own API key (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) or one you
  run yourself (for example Ollama). A line under the box always says which model is answering.
- **Query.** The same engine by hand, with about 70 fields, conditions, sorting, per-artist, per-album and per-release-year caps, and
  date scopes.
- **Tools.** Time Machine, Similar Sounds, Fading Favorites, The Climb, Rabbit Hole, Album Turnovers, Chart Toppers, Missing Data,
  Library Gaps, Loved Status Audit, Tag Management, a Collage Generator, and more.
- **Your own corrections.** Fix spellings, release dates, tempos and artwork on your Mac, and back them up and restore them.
- **Offline mode** stops every online lookup and keeps everything already on your Mac working.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
- A free Last.fm API key (the Welcome screen walks you through getting one).
- Optional: the Music app, for the features that look at your Music library. The on-device Ask model needs macOS 26 or later.

## Install

1. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.0.dmg` below.
2. Open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**.
3. Open Scroblytics and follow the Welcome screen.

The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS only asks the usual "downloaded from the internet" question the first time. To
verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.0.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.0.dmg`).

## Good to know

- **The first import can take a while.** Last.fm hands over 200 scrobbles per request, so a long history takes minutes to hours. You can
  use the app while it runs, and it picks up where it stopped if you quit.
- **Artwork, tags, popularity and release dates fill in slowly** in the background after the import, to stay within the free services' limits.
- **The on-device Ask model is limited.** It understands fewer kinds of request and often misreads them, and it can't do "similar to…"
  requests. A larger model through your own key is far more accurate. Always check the filters Ask shows you.
- **Music permission.** The first time a feature reads or changes your Music library, macOS asks whether Scroblytics may control Music.
  The app changes Music only when you ask: creating a playlist, or removing entries from Favorites after you confirm.
- **No automatic updates yet.** Check this page for new versions.
- **Track Actions** (loving tracks and editing tags from the app) change your public Last.fm profile, and are off until you connect them.

## Privacy

Everything lives in a database on your Mac. The app contacts only the services it needs (Last.fm, Deezer, GetSongBPM, MusicBrainz and
the Ask provider you choose). Read the [privacy policy](https://github.com/scrobl/Scroblytics/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
