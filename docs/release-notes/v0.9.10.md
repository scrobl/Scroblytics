# Scroblytics 0.9.10 (beta)

Fixes for Rabbit Hole, tag sync and unreadable-database errors.  Plus a few small enhancements such as: Per-year results in Query and Ask and a profile picture option for shared cards

## New: results for each calendar year

Query and Ask can now show the top artists, albums or tracks **for each calendar year of your history**: "my most played track of each year", "top 3 albums from every year". In Query, turn on **Break down by calendar year** in the scope section, choose how many to keep per year with **Top (per year)**, and optionally **Newest year first**. Each row carries its year. Ask understands the same requests in plain words.

## New: choose the order of the results

Query has a **Show results by** menu under Sort. Sort still decides *which* results are kept (the top by plays, for instance); Show results by decides how they are *listed*. With an "at most N per release year" cap, the default is now release year ascending, so "the top album from each release year" reads as a timeline instead of a ranking.

## New: your profile picture on shared cards

**Settings › General › Sharing**, the Share preview and the Collage Generator have an **Include my profile picture** option (shown with your name, so turn on **Show my Last.fm name** first). It puts your Last.fm profile picture, as a circle, in front of your name. The picture is fetched from Last.fm the first time, kept on your Mac and refreshed about once a week; with Offline mode on, or if Last.fm can't be reached, the card uses the saved picture or goes without one and says so. It is off by default, and nothing about it leaves your Mac unless you paste or send the card.

## Fixed: "In Apple Music library" in Rabbit Hole and Similar Sounds

A condition such as **In Apple Music library = Yes** matched nothing in Rabbit Hole and Similar Sounds, so a seed could show no suggestions even though the same track had plenty without the condition. It now filters against your Music library as it does in Query.

## Fixed: tag sync stopping on one artist

If Last.fm couldn't answer for a single artist, the whole refresh of your personal tags stopped, and tried again with the same result at every launch. That artist is now skipped and the rest are kept. A failure that affects everything (offline, a rate limit, a key problem, or most artists failing) still stops it, with a message.

## Fixed: clearer errors when the database can't be read

If macOS stops Scroblytics from reading or writing its own database (a full disk, a second copy of the app, a damaged or moved file), the app could show an absurd scrobble count and a vague message. A count it can't read is now treated as an error, and the message says what is likely wrong in plain words with a code to quote. **Help › Report a Problem…** now also includes the database size and the free disk space, still with no music or account details.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.10.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.10.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.10.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
