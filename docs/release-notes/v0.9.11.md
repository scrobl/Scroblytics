# Scroblytics 0.9.11 (beta)

Weather, seasons and places: see how the weather and where you were shape what you listen to. Plus new share cards for every Trends chart.

## New: weather and listening

Turn on **Settings › Weather**, choose where you live, and the app downloads the hourly weather for that place and matches it to every play by the moment you played it. Then you can ask what you play in rain, snow, fog, a storm, clear or cloudy weather, and when it is cold, mild or hot. "Cold" and "hot" are relative to your own climate (the coldest and warmest 20% of your hours by default, both editable), so 15° can be hot in one place and cold in another. Temperatures show in °C or °F (it follows your Mac's region unless you choose).

- **Query and Ask:** new play filters (a weather, a temperature band or an exact range) and new fields: Weather, Temperature, Average temperature, Share of plays in a weather and Weather lift. Ask understands them in plain words ("what do I play on cold rainy nights?") with hosted and local models.
- **Detail pages and Scrobble History:** artist and album pages have a Weather tile (the weather you play it in, against your usual), and a track's plays can show the weather and the place of each play.
- **Seasons:** the play filter has months, with Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter chips (for your hemisphere, set in Settings), and a new Season field says which season an artist, album or track peaks in.

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, which needs no account. Only the coordinates of your place, rounded to about 11 km, and a date range are sent: never anything about your music. It is downloaded once, kept on your Mac, and topped up as you listen. It is off until you turn it on. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

## New: trips and places

Add trips under **Settings › Weather › Trips**: a place and the dates you were there. For those dates the weather is the weather where you were, and every play in that time is filed under that city, without touching any scrobble. Pick from places you have used before instead of searching each time, and the end date starts from the start date. **City** is also a Query field and a play filter, so you can ask for "what I played in Lisbon".

## New: Trends › Weather & places

A new group in Trends, with six cards:

- **Weather and listening:** a tile for each kind of weather, with how much more or less than an average day you listen in it.
- **Leaning into the weather:** the artists, albums or tracks you play more (or less) than usual in a chosen weather, allowing for the time of year, and only with enough plays on enough different days that one rainy afternoon is not mistaken for a pattern.
- **Artist weather map:** your artists on a map of weather against temperature. Each artist is its picture, sized by plays, ringed by how strongly it leans there, and placed where its plays lean furthest against the usual (or, in **Most played**, where it is played most). Top 10, 25, 50 or 100. Hover for an artist's weather spread; click to open those plays in Query.
- **Temperature and time of day:** how much more or less than usual you play at each temperature in each part of the day.
- **Listening against the weather:** each week's (or day's) scrobbles over its temperature, with rainy and snowy weeks marked.
- **Where you listened:** your trips against home, with plays a day, new artists, the weather and top artists, in a colour for each place. Switch between **City**, **State/Region** and **Country** to group repeat trips together.

Click a tile, cell, bubble or week to open exactly those plays in Query.

## New: share cards drawn for the card

Every Trends chart's **Share** button now makes a card drawn at the card's size instead of a picture of the window: larger text, the chart filling the space, and a sentence about what it shows that you can retype. Each card has include/exclude options that make sense for it (the key, the artwork, how many rows, the shading and so on), and the comparison with the previous period is off unless you turn it on. Weather cards can also show the weather of a play.

## Improved: very large libraries

The weather features were timed on a library of over 360,000 plays spanning 17 years: each step takes a few seconds at most, and Trends cards recompute faster. Saving a share card as a PNG no longer holds on to memory after each export.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.11.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.11.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.11.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
