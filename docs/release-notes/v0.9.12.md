# Scroblytics 0.9.12 (beta)

A syncing fix for people whose app sometimes seemed to re-sync everything when they opened it, plus a clearer choice before any long re-check.

## Fixed: late plays and deletions no longer trigger a whole-history re-sync

Each time you open the app it checks Last.fm for plays newer than the newest one it has. But plays that reach Last.fm late, stamped with their original (older) time, which happens with scrobblers and devices that send plays in batches or while offline, are older than that and can't be seen that way. The app notices when Last.fm's total play count rises by more than it found (or falls, because plays were deleted), and until now it answered by re-reading your **entire** history, which on a big library takes many minutes and looks like the app starting over every time.

Now it first re-checks only the last couple of weeks before your previous sync (Last.fm only accepts plays up to about two weeks old, so that is where late plays and recent deletions are) and compares the totals again. If they agree, that's all it does: usually a few seconds.

## New: you choose before a long re-check

If the totals still disagree after that (a play deleted on Last.fm long ago, say), the app no longer starts the long walk by itself. The sync finishes and a strip under the toolbar says how much Last.fm differs, the likely reason, and roughly how long a full check would take, with three choices:

- **Check now** reads your whole history and reconciles it.
- **Ignore** accepts the difference and stops asking, until you run a Full Resync.
- **Skip for now** asks again at the next sync. So does closing the app without answering.

## Improved: Report a Problem shows what kind of sync ran

The activity log (**Help › Report a Problem…**) now records for each sync whether it was a quick check, a first import, a resumed import or a full resync, and, when a quick sync turned into a re-check, why, with counts only and nothing about your music. If syncing ever seems to start over, the summary can now say what it did.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.12.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.12.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.12.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
