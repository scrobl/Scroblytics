Query can now list your individual scrobbles and your listening sessions, trips get a tool of their own that works with or without weather, and you can filter by state or country.

## New: Scrobbles in Query
Under **Find**, **Scrobbles** lists single plays instead of artists, albums or tracks: one row for each listen. 
- **About the play:** when it was played (with the hour, day of the week, part of the day and season), the city, state or region and country you were in and the weather at that moment, and how long after the play before it.
- **About the track:** which time you have played it ("Listen # of this track") and whether it is the first. The fields of the track, album and artist a play is of appear as **Track · Plays**, **Album · Release year**, **Artist · Tags** and so on, so "plays of songs I have played fewer than 5 times" or "plays of 1990s albums" are conditions.
- **Open the plays behind a number:** clicking an hour or a day in Overview's "Last 24 Hours" and streak strips, or a trip in Tools › Trips, now opens exactly those plays in Query, one row for each, instead of a list of tracks. Back returns to the page you came from.

## New: Sessions in Query
Under **Find**, **Sessions** lists listening sessions: one row for each sitting, a run of plays each started within 30 minutes of the one before (10 to 120 minutes under **Scope & limit › A session ends after**). A row is named by when the session began, with a line saying what it was ("182 plays · 9h 2m · 41 artists · mostly one artist") and the artwork of its most played artist. You can show and filter on its length, plays, artists and most played artist, the song it opened and closed with, the day and hour it began, the time since the previous session, and where it began and what the weather was. Clicking a row opens exactly that session's plays, in the order you played them. A period or a play filter picks sessions by when and where they began.
On a Scrobbles query, session fields tell each play where it sits in its session: whether it starts or ends one, how long the session lasted, and how many plays in a row are by the same artist.

## New: Ask understands plays and sessions
With the larger models (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Ollama or a server on this Mac), Ask can answer with plays or sessions: "what did I listen to on March 8, 2025", "my last 20 plays", "everything I played after midnight in 2024", "songs I played for the very first time this year", "my 10 longest listening sessions", "sessions where I played more than 50 songs", "plays of loved songs while I was in Germany". Ranking songs, albums or artists ("top songs") works as before. The on-device model is very limited and does not know plays or sessions.  For best results use a OpenAI or Anthropic API key.

## New: Trips tool
Trips now live in one place, **Tools › Trips**. It lists every trip with its plays, plays a day against your home rate, the artists you first heard there, the weather (when weather is on) and your top artists, with search and sorting, **I'm Away Now…**, **Add a Trip…**, **Back Home**, edit and remove. Trips that share days are highlighted in orange, and clicking a trip opens its plays in Query. Settings › Weather keeps a pointer to it.

## Improved: Trips work without weather
A trip no longer needs weather turned on. With weather off, a place is just a name you type (nothing is searched or sent anywhere), and your home place is optional: without one, the days you were not on a trip are called **Home**. Trends' **Where you listened** card shows with or without the weather columns, and its "Showing 8 of N" line links to the full list in the tool.

## New: Filter by state or region and country
Under **Scope & limit › Only count plays at certain times or in certain weather**, **In this state or region** and **In this country** join **In this city**: "top 25 songs I listened to in Germany", "the plays I made in Illinois". They work in Ask too. A state or country is known when the place was chosen from the city search; a place you only typed a name for has none.

## Improved: Weather catches up faster
Weather was topped up at most twice a day, so plays from the last few hours showed no weather. It is now topped up when a sync finishes and shortly after the app opens, at most every 15 minutes.

## Fixed: Tracks named with only symbols
A track whose title has no Latin letter or digit, such as one named "/=/", was treated as having no name when the app matched plays to an album's tracklist: the track table, the turnover and the coverage got it wrong. Such titles (and titles in another alphabet) now match their own entry on the tracklist. The turnover of an affected album refreshes at your next sync.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.13.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.13.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.13.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
