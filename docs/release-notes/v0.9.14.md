Similar Sounds and Rabbit Hole can now suggest music you have never played, and query/Ask results can now be shared as a collage.

## New: Include new music in Similar Sounds and Rabbit Hole
Both tools can now offer songs from outside your history. In **Similar Sounds** tick **Include new music**; in **Rabbit Hole**, open **Style** and choose **New music: A little or Half**. Both are off by default and remembered for each tool.
- **Blended in, marked "New":** tracks Last.fm calls similar that you have never played appear in the same list with a small **New** badge. Clicking one opens its Last.fm page. A track you have played under a slightly different title (a remaster, a "feat." tag) is not counted as new.
- **Similar Sounds:** for a song, the new tracks are the similar songs Last.fm names that you haven't played, placed among your own by match score (at random places under Shuffled, after your own under Play count and Last played). For an artist or album, they are the best-known songs of the closest similar artists. The summary line says how many are new.
- **Rabbit Hole:** "A little" makes about one suggestion in four new, "Half" about one in two. Cards carry the badge and a "New to you" reason, and a path can continue from a new track.
- **Conditions:** a condition describes your own listening, so while one is set, new tracks are left out and the tool says so.
- **Apple Music playlist export** adds a new track only if it is already in your Music library.

## New: Collage from Query's results
Query's results header has a **Collage** button for Artists, Albums and Tracks queries. It opens the Collage Generator with exactly those results, in the order the query put them, each tile showing the value of the column the results are sorted by (plays, last played, release year...), in the smallest square grid that holds them (up to 144 tiles). The caption names the query. **Use Top Items Instead** returns the tool to its own choices, and Back returns to the query, which keeps its navigation.

## New: Collage of a saved query, and a caption of your own
The Collage Generator has a third source, **A saved query**: pick one of your saved Query sets and the collage is its rows. A new **Caption** box puts a line of your own in the strip under the collage in place of the automatic one. The display controls now run the full width of the window, with Save PNG, Copy Image and Share on a row of their own.

## Improved: Collage zoom
The magnifying glasses beside the zoom slider step the zoom by 0.1, and the Fit button no longer gets squeezed when the row is narrow.

## Fixed: Editions counted on their own in Chart Toppers and the Collage
An album edition you chose to **count on its own** is now a row of its own in Chart Toppers and in the Collage's release-period source, with only its own plays. A combined album is named by its plain name when it was scrobbled, else by its most played edition.

## Fixed: Your own artwork on Album Turnovers
Album Turnovers rows (and the Overview card) now show artwork you set yourself, then the detected cover, then the scrobbles' cover, the same order as Top Charts, instead of the placeholder.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.14.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.14.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.14.dmg`). The app tells you about this version with a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**.
