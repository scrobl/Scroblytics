New: Set custom listening Goals and track their progress with a burndown chart with predictions based on recent habits, or focused listening.

## New: Custom Goals
Tools › **Goals** lists your goals as cards. Press **New Goal…**, name it and choose the first day and the deadline, for example "hear every track I've loved between now and the end of the year".
- **What counts:** Set your goal by loved status, artists, and tags. A track is counted when it has a play on or after the goal's first day. 
- **The cards:** how many you have heard, a bar with a tick where a steady pace to the deadline would have you today, whether you are ahead of or behind that plan, the tracks left, the days left and the pace needed. A line under the bar says where Your habits ends up if you keep listening as you have lately, for example "About 15 short at your 90-day pace" or "On pace to finish Nov 20". Cards are green when ahead, orange when behind (or ahead but short at your pace), red once the deadline has passed with tracks left, and gold once achieved.
- **The goal's page:** a burn-down chart of the tracks still to hear, with three forecasts of how it goes from here (Your habits, Shuffle your favorites, Unplayed first), each run 300 times, with the chance of finishing by the deadline and the dates by which half and nine tenths of what is left will have been heard. It works out the fewest plays a day, on top of your habits, to give to tracks you haven't heard to be 90% sure of finishing, and you can set that share yourself. Assumptions you can change: how far back to learn your pace from, whether to expect more loves before the deadline, and whether to count only loved tracks that are in your Apple Music library.
- **Still to hear:** the tracks that remain, with artwork and the usual flag, search, filters, sorting and the usual track menu. Create Apple Music Playlist makes a playlist of the tracks the list shows, and Set Aside from This Goal takes a track you don't want to chase out of the numbers.
- **On the Overview and to share:** up to three goals can sit in a row of cards on the Overview, and any goal can be shared as an image or text from its page or its card.
- Goals are worked out from your history each time, so they need no upkeep. Only the goal itself (name, dates and assumptions) is saved, and it is part of your backups. See the **Goals** article in Help for the details.

## One thing to know: loved dates
To estimate how many new loves to expect, Goals uses the dates you loved tracks on Last.fm. The first sync after updating walks your loved tracks once to save those dates; until it has, every loved track counts from the first day and new loves can't be estimated.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.15.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.15.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.15.dmg`). The app tells you about this version with a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**.
