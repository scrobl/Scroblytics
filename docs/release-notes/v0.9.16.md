New: Auto-updating Apple Music playlists, a Release Year Audit that compares your album dates with Apple Music, and rolling date conditions in Query and Ask.

## New: Auto-updating playlists
Make a playlist in Apple Music from a list in Scroblytics and keep it up to date. When you create a playlist from Top Charts, a Query, Most Recent, Most Often, Chart Toppers, Flagged Tracks, a goal's still-to-hear list, an artist's Tracks list or Missing Data (tracks), switch on **Keep this playlist updated after each sync**. After every sync the playlist is refreshed.
- **How a refresh works:** Music's scripting can only remove tracks and add them to the end of a playlist, so a refresh keeps the top of the list that still matches, removes the tail and adds the rest. A goal's list, where tracks drop out of the middle as you hear them, removes just those tracks and leaves the others where they are.
- **Tools › Create › Auto-Updating Playlists** lists them all with their status, **Refresh Now**, **Show in Music**, a switch to pause one, **Stop Updating**, and **Make It Again** for one that was deleted in Music. You can add one from a Top Charts preset or a saved track Query.
- **Safe by design:** Music has to be open (Scroblytics never starts it), an empty result never empties a playlist, a deleted or smart playlist is reported rather than remade, and tracks that aren't in your Apple Music library are skipped and counted. Only playlists you switched on are ever touched.
- Each playlist's recipe (what it is made from, not its tracks) is part of your backups. See the **Auto-Updating Playlists** article in Help.

## New: Release Year Audit
Tools › Organize › **Release Year Audit** lists the albums whose release year in Scroblytics differs from the year in your Apple Music library, with where each Scroblytics date came from (your own edit, MusicBrainz, Deezer or inferred).
- **For each album:** **Keep Scroblytics**, **Edit…** or **Use Apple Music's**, singly or in bulk after a confirmation. Filter by size of the gap (1–2 years, 3+ years), your own dates or compilations, and sort by difference. Decisions are remembered, can be undone from the Decisions list and are included in backups. A kept album returns if either year changes.
- **Compilations:** a compilation lists the tracks whose year differs from Music's and can set each track's own date to Music's, singly or in bulk. A date you set yourself is never changed. When an album's tracks are spread evenly over several years, the album's year agrees with any of them.
- **Update Apple Music…:** after you keep your date or edit one, a prompt offers to set the Year of the album's tracks in Music to match and lists exactly what would change. You confirm every time. Each track is found by its Music ID and left alone if its name or year has changed since it was read, and a compilation's tracks keep their own years.
- Only Scroblytics' own dates change unless you choose Update Apple Music. See the **Release Year Audit** article in Help.

## New: rolling and weekday date conditions
Date fields in Query and Ask gain **in the last** and **not in the last** (a number of days, weeks, months or years counted back from today, so a saved query keeps rolling), **not on**, and **day of week is / is not**. Ask also understands "release date year" as a release-year cap.

## Smaller changes
- Goals show **heard today** on a running goal's card and on its page.
- The album track table shows a **Released** column when a track has a release date of its own that differs from the album's.
- Most Recent › View all uses the app's artwork lookup, so plays no longer show Last.fm's blank star.
- Backup Help now lists everything a backup holds, and a new check makes sure future features that store your own data are added to backups.
- A fix for a warning at launch about saving from a background thread when automatic playlists listened for changes.
- The contact address in Report a Problem, the README and the privacy policy is now the project's new address.

## One thing to know: Apple Music permission
Automatic playlists and Update Apple Music change things in your Music library, only when you ask. The first time, macOS asks whether Scroblytics may control Music.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.16.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.16.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.16.dmg`). The app tells you about this version with a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**.
