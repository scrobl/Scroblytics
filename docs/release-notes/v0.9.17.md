New: Unscrobbled Plays finds songs Apple Music played that never reached Last.fm and can scrobble them, a warning when your scrobbler stops, and Songs Without Albums.

## New: Unscrobbled Plays
Tools › Organize › **Unscrobbled Plays** lists the songs Apple Music has played more recently than their latest scrobble, so they were probably played without being scrobbled. Music keeps one last-played date for each song, and a scrobbler normally gives the scrobble that same moment, so a later date in Music means a probable miss.
- **Not scrobbled:** songs with no scrobble at all and songs played after their last scrobble, with Music's last play, the latest scrobble and how many plays each has. Filter by when Music last played the song (any time, 12 months, 90 days, 30 days or 14 days) and sort by last played, largest gap or artist. When the misses bunch up in a few months, the headline names them.
- **Match spelling variants:** on by default, a song scrobbled as "Title (feat. …)" or "(Acoustic)" counts as the same song, as everywhere else in the app. Turn it off to list songs whose scrobbles carry a different spelling, with how they were scrobbled, which helps if you want to clean those up.
## New: scrobble the missed plays
With Track Actions connected, **Scrobble…** sends the missed plays that Last.fm will still take. Last.fm reportedly accepts a scrobble only for a play from about the last 14 days, so those rows are marked **Can be scrobbled** and the button counts them.
- **You confirm the exact list first.** A review lists every play with a checkbox, so you can untick any. Each is sent with the time Music last played the song, under the name and album Music has. Nothing is sent until you press the button.
- **Plays go out in batches of 50, oldest first.** Afterwards it says how many Last.fm took and lists any it ignored, with the reason. A play already sent is remembered for a while so it isn't offered again before the next sync brings it into your history, and a sync runs afterwards.
- **If the connection drops before Last.fm answers,** it can't know whether the last batch was taken. The review says so, and you should sync and read Music again before sending those a second time.
## New: a warning when your scrobbler stops
Off until you turn it on, in **Settings › Sync › Scrobbling check** or with the checkbox in Unscrobbled Plays. After each sync it looks at the songs Music played in the last few days. If at least **3 different songs in the last 7 days** are missing from your history (you can set 1 to 20 songs and 1, 3, 7 or 14 days), a strip under the toolbar says how many and since when, and how many can still be scrobbled. **Review** opens Unscrobbled Plays on the last 14 days, **Dismiss** keeps it away until scrobbling has recovered and the problem returns, and **Turn Off** switches the check off.
- It runs only while Music is open and only if you have allowed Scroblytics to control Music, reads just the recently played songs (about a second and a half), compares them on your Mac.

## New: Songs Without Albums
Tools › Organize › **Songs Without Albums** has two read-only lists. **No album** is songs whose Album is empty in Music and scrobbles Last.fm recorded with no album name, each with the other albums the song is known on. **Alone on an album** is albums that hold exactly one song, told apart by album artist so the tracks of a compilation or a guest credit don't each look like an album of their own.
- **Hide singles** is on by default: a one-song album is often a real single. A release counts as a single when its tracklist is known to have one track (a tracklist you pasted, an album you matched in Fix Album Match, or a Last.fm tracklist the app already has), or, when no tracklist is known, when the album is named after its song. A known tracklist of several tracks always wins, and those albums are marked as only partly in your library.
- It also points out albums that look like another edition of one you already have. See the **Songs Without Albums** article in Help.

## Smaller changes
- Release Year Audit: **Compilations** is now **Mixed years**, with "more than", "fewer than" and "exactly" conditions on how many years an album's tracks span and how many of its tracks differ. It also gains a Plays column and sort, and the **Update Apple Music** prompt has a Cancel button.
- Fix Album Match is only offered on an album page when there is a match to fix. Before, an album with no scrobbles opened an empty sheet with no way to close it.
- The header controls of Unscrobbled Plays and Songs Without Albums spread across the window and wrap as it narrows, instead of stacking one per row.
- Help, Settings and the privacy policy now say that Track Actions can also scrobble plays.

## Two things to know
- **Apple Music permission:** Unscrobbled Plays and its optional check read your Music library, and Songs Without Albums does too. The first time, macOS asks whether Scroblytics may control Music. Nothing in Music is changed.
- **Scrobbling writes to Last.fm,** only when you confirm it, and needs Track Actions (Settings › Sync). Read the list in the review before you press the button.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.17.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.17.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.17.dmg`). The app tells you about this version with a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**.
