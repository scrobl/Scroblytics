New: Apple Music library details (date added, genre, tempo and length), settings that decide which title notes make a different song, and a batch of fixes.

## New: Apple Music library details
Off until you turn it on, in **Settings › Sync › Apple Music library details**. It reads each song's **date added**, **genre**, **tempo** and **length** from the Music app and keeps them in a small file beside the database (not in the database or in backups). **Forget What Was Read** deletes the file.
- **Query:** new fields **Date added to Apple Music** and **Apple Music genre** for songs, albums and artists (for albums and artists, the earliest date added and the most common genre).
- **Pages:** song, album and artist pages show **Added to Music** and **Genre** tiles. When a page says an album or song isn't in the library, the tile has a button that reads Music again.
- **Taste chart:** a **Source** control switches the bars between Tags, Apple Music genre and Both. A genre that is also a tag counts once per song, and a bar's click-through opens Query on exactly what the bar counted.
- **Tempo and length:** Music's tempo tag is used where nothing was found online and nothing was entered. Music's length fills in where Deezer had nothing and nothing was entered, when the copies in your library agree to within 2 seconds. Those songs are never looked up online. A new setting, off by default, lets Apple Music's length win over the one found online.
- **How songs are matched:** a Last.fm song is matched to a library song only by identical artist and title, or by a differently spelled title that pairs one to one. When the library holds a song more than once (a studio album, a compilation, a live record), the copy on the album you scrobbled it under is used. Where there are several possible partners nothing is taken, and Settings lists those songs.
- **When it reads:** after a sync, and only while Music is already open. It checks the song count first, and does a full read (about 9 seconds, in the background) when the count changed or the last read is over 12 hours old.

## New: Song matching settings
**Settings › General › Song matching** has three switches that decide when two titles count as the same song:
- **Remasters and other edition notes:** on by default, as before. "Song (Remastered)", "Song - Mono" and "Song (Deluxe Edition)" are matched with "Song". Off, each title is its own song.
- **Instrumental and acoustic versions:** off by default. "Song (Instrumental)" and "Song (Acoustic)" are different recordings, so they are different songs. On, they are matched with "Song".
- **Explicit and clean versions:** on by default, as before. Off, "Song [Explicit]" and "Song (Clean)" are different songs.

Case, punctuation, "&" versus "and" and "feat." credits are always ignored. The choice applies to Library Gaps, the Loved Status Audit, playlist export, Tag Management, track lengths, New Music and the Release Year Audit. Comparing an album's official tracklist with the titles you scrobbled for it always ignores these notes, because the two come from different services, and Multi-Album Tracks keeps its own matching. If you would rather correct a title in Music or on Last.fm than have the app guess, turn the switches off and the places where two spellings might be the same song show as uncertain for you to decide. The Help article **How songs are matched** explains each.
- **Tag Management:** a playlist track such as "Song (Instrumental)" was matched to the scrobbled "Song", so tagging it would have put the tag on the vocal version, because Last.fm tags a title. While instrumental and acoustic versions are kept apart that can no longer happen.

## Fixed and changed
- **Missing Data pictures:** rows showed a grey tile for items whose detail page had a picture, because they used only the picture saved with the scrobbles, which is often Last.fm's blank placeholder. They now pick the picture the way a detail page does.
- **Release years per album:** a song on two albums is two versions with their own years, but a year set for one applied to the other. A track's year saved by the Release Year Audit now applies only under the album it was set for, and the audit no longer counts one album's year as your own date on another. Years the audit saved earlier are tied once to the album of the decision that set them. A year typed on a track's own page still applies on any album.
- **Per-release-year cap:** "At most N per release year" let through every song with no known release year, so a "one per year" list gained all the songs still waiting for a date. They are left out until a year is found.
- **Auto-updating playlists:** a refresh read the playlist back right after changing it and reported "didn't match after the update" whenever Music returned the songs in another order, then added the same songs again. The read-back is repeated before it counts, and when Music holds exactly the wanted songs in another order the refresh succeeds and the row says the order shown may differ.
- **Ask:** "most recently added" now means a date-added window (such as the last 90 days) with the list sorted by plays, instead of a second sort that only broke ties.
- **Rankings chart:** each line starts with the item's picture in a circle ringed in the line's colour: an artist's picture, an album's cover, or a track's own picture or else its artist's.
- **Released tile:** the month is shortened ("Nov 10, 2026") to match the other date tiles. The release-date editor and the Release Year Audit keep the full month.
- **Tool headers:** the controls of Library Gaps, Missing Data, Loved Status Audit, Release Year Audit, Fading Favorites, Flagged Tracks, Most Recent Most Often, The Climb, Time Machine, Trips, Album Turnovers, Goals, Rabbit Hole, Similar Sounds and Auto-Updating Playlists spread across the window and wrap as it narrows.

## Two things to know
- **Apple Music permission:** Apple Music library details reads your Music library, so the first time macOS asks whether Scroblytics may control Music. It is off until you turn it on, and nothing in Music is changed.
- **Instrumental and acoustic versions are now separate songs by default.** Before, they were matched with the plain title. If you liked that, turn on **Count instrumental and acoustic versions as the same song** in Settings › General. Library Gaps rebuilds its list at the next **Check library**.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.18.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.18.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.18.dmg`). The app tells you about this version with a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**.
