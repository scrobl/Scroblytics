New: Artist Map, a picture of how your artists relate, and Duplicate Scrobbles, which finds plays of a song that are too close together to be real. Also a "…" button for the right-click menu, an installer window that shows where to drag the app, and a few fixes.

## New: Artist Map (Tools › Discover)
Your artists as dots, joined by how similar Last.fm says they are. A closer pair of dots means a stronger match, a larger dot means more plays, and artists that cluster are given a group colour and a name.
- **Top artists:** your top 10, 25, 50, 100 or 200 artists for a period, using the same period list as Overview (Last 30 days by default). A **Minimum match** slider hides the weaker links, and a switch adds artists you have never played that Last.fm lists as similar.
- **From a seed:** pick one artist and see the artists in your library most similar to it around it, one or two steps out. Artists with no link left at the current minimum leave the map instead of sitting alone.
- **Pictures, pan and zoom:** artist pictures on the dots (optional), drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and the map and its side panel grow with the window. Click a dot to see its closest links, with the score in each direction, and to open its page, start a map from it or open it in Query. Last.fm's scores are not symmetric, so the map draws the higher of the two.
- **Groups:** the panel says "In a group with Artist A and 11 others" (or that the artist is the busiest of its group), the selected dot shows its group's colour, and links into other groups are marked.
- **Lookups:** Last.fm is asked for each artist's similar artists once, six at a time, with a Stop button, and the answers are saved on your Mac. Artists that have no saved answer yet are fetched when you press the lookup button beside the status text; choosing a seed counts as that request. Answers older than 30 days keep drawing the map and are offered as **Refresh**. Offline mode uses only what is saved, and a failed or offline lookup is never saved as "no similar artists".
- The Help article explains what the distances and colours mean and what leaves your Mac (artist names go to Last.fm, as in Similar Sounds).

## New: Duplicate Scrobbles (Tools › Organize)
Finds plays of one song that started closer together than the song could last, which is what a double submit looks like, so you can delete the extras on Last.fm.
- **One row per song,** with each run of close plays under it, a **Certain** or **Possible** badge for each run, and the plays with their times. The row shows the song's length and closest gap, and has Query, Last.fm and Ignore buttons. Sort by latest run, extra plays or smallest gap.
- **Compare with Apple Music:** reads your Music library once (Music must already be open; nothing is changed) and shows each song's scrobbles beside Apple Music's play count, and whether they agree, there are more scrobbles than plays, Music has more plays, or the song isn't in the library. A filter keeps one of those groups. It is a clue and does not change Certain or Possible.
- **Ignore:** the eye button moves a run you have reviewed to List › Ignored, where the same button puts it back. **Ignore All Shown…** does the whole list after a confirmation. A run that later gains another play comes back for review.

## "…" button for the right-click menu
The right-click menu on artwork holds many of the app's actions but nothing hinted that it was there. Artwork in Top Charts, Library, the artist page's album and track grids, Overview's top picks and the detail pages now shows a "…" badge in its corner when you point at it or tab to it, and it opens the same menu. Artist, album and song pages also get an **Actions "…"** button beside Share.

## Installer window
Opening the downloaded disk image now shows **Scroblytics** on the left, **Applications** on the right and an arrow between them, instead of Finder sorting them alphabetically.

## Fixed and changed
- **A song on two albums:** opening a song from an album's track list showed the plays from every album the song is on, so a song on an EP you had never played it on opened a page full of the other album's plays. A song opened from an album's track list now shows that album's plays first, with a **This album | All albums** switch and a line saying where the rest of the plays are. Opened from anywhere else, the page is unchanged.
- **Ask and Query, last played under a day or time filter:** a condition on Last played, First played or the days-since fields used only the plays a day or time filter kept, so "not played since April" under a Saturday filter ignored plays on other days. Under such a filter these now read all time (an explicit scope is kept).
- **Dates of plays include the year** in the Query table, result cards and copied text. Overview's recent plays still leave it off for this year.
- **Query columns:** a column that is limited to a date range, with no comparison, can now go back to the query's own period.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.19.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.19.dmg.sha256`.
