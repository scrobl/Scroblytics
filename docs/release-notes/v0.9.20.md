New: where your artists are from. Scroblytics now looks up the city, state or region and country of each artist, and uses it in a world map, a "close to home" chart, a country passport, Query, the Timeline, Goals and more. Also a heads-up about Last.fm API keys.

## Heads-up: Last.fm now requires Pro for API keys
From 1 December 2026 Last.fm only accepts an API key whose owner has a **Last.fm Pro** subscription. Scroblytics doesn't ship a key: you register your own, so the Last.fm account you registered it under needs Pro, or Last.fm refuses the key and syncing stops until it subscribes. Scroblytics itself is free and stays free; the subscription is Last.fm's, and Last.fm sets its price and terms.
- The Welcome screen, Settings › Sync, Help and the README now say so, and no longer call the key free.
- If Last.fm rejects your key (error 10 or 26), the message now says a lapsed Pro subscription can be the cause, instead of only suggesting a typo.
- Nothing already saved on your Mac is affected. Your history, edits and settings stay where they are.

## New: where artists are from
- **Looked up for you:** the city, state or region and country of each artist, from MusicBrainz, most-played artists first, one request at a time in the background. It shows as "artist origins" on the status strip, pauses in Offline mode, and can be switched off (and the looked-up places forgotten, keeping your own corrections) in Settings › Sync. Artist names go to MusicBrainz, as release-date lookups already do, and the answers are saved on your Mac.
- **Artist page:** a tile says "Born in", "Formed in" or "From", with a flag on a best guess that nobody has checked. The pencil opens a sheet to correct the city, state or region and country by hand, reset to the detected place, confirm a guess, or pick a different MusicBrainz artist when a namesake was chosen. Opening an artist's page moves it to the front of the queue.
- **Query and Ask:** five Origin fields for artists (city, state or region, country, the whole place, and whether a guess is waiting to be checked). Albums and tracks use their artist's place, your own correction wins over the lookup, and Query's Resolve button moves the artists a result is waiting on to the front. Ask knows the fields.
- **Missing Data:** **Artist Origin** (artists with none yet) and **Origin to Review** (best guesses, with a **Looks Right** button). Clicking a row opens the correction sheet.
- **Backup:** your corrections go in a backup; looked-up places don't, since they can be fetched again. Older backups restore as before.
- The Help article "Where artists are from" explains Born in versus Formed in, how the state or region is chosen and what leaves your Mac.

## New: where your music is from (Trends › Taste & eras)
- **Origin map:** a world map drawn inside the app, with countries shaded by plays and a dot for each city. Each dot carries the picture of its top artist (a dashed ring where only a region or country is known), and hovering shows the top three artists. Click a place to open its artists in Query. A **Show** menu limits the map to the top 10, 25, 50 or 100 artists of the period, and the plays that are not on the map are counted so the totals still add up. Pan, and zoom far enough (up to 80×) to pull apart places close together in a big city; the smaller of two overlapping places is drawn on top, and at deep zoom the places on screen are named without printing over each other.
- **Close to home:** how much of your listening is by artists from your own city, region and country, by year. A Weather home saved before countries were kept now gets its country (and region) worked out on your Mac; if home still has none, the chart asks for one.
- **Passport:** every country you have heard, with the day you first heard an artist from it and who.
- Everything is drawn from data bundled in the app (country outlines from Natural Earth, public domain; about 70,000 cities from GeoNames, CC BY 4.0, credited in Help and the README), so the maps work offline and nothing about your listening leaves your Mac.

## Also using where artists are from
- **Artist Map:** a **Color by Country** option, the selected artist's place, and groups described by the country most of their members are from.
- **Rabbit Hole:** a **Places** option (stay in the scene, or cross borders), with the reason shown on each suggestion.
- **Trips:** each trip says what share of its plays were by artists from the country you were in.
- **Timeline:** a month shows a "First artist from" row of flags for the countries whose first artist you played that month.
- **Goals:** a goal can be limited to artists from some countries, combined with loved status, artists and tags. Tracks whose artist hasn't been looked up yet are not counted, and the goal page says how many are waiting.

## Fixed
- **Back to Query from an artist page:** after opening an artist from Query results, Back and the "Query" crumb could do nothing. They now return to the results.
- The origin correction sheet can be closed with Escape, and has a Cancel button while it loads.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a Last.fm API key, which now needs Last.fm Pro on the account that registered it (see above). Download `Scroblytics-0.9.20.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.20.dmg.sha256`.
