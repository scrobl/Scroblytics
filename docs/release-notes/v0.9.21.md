Scroblytics now comes with its own Last.fm API key, so a username is all it needs. You no longer register a key (or pay for Last.fm Pro) just to read your own history.

## No more API key to register
Last.fm now requires a **Last.fm Pro** subscription on whoever owns an API key. Until now every Scroblytics user owned a key, so every user would have needed Pro. From this version the app has its own key (and shared secret) built in, and the developer holds the Pro subscription it needs. Scroblytics stays free.
- **New users:** the welcome screen asks for your Last.fm username and nothing else.
- **Track Actions** (loving tracks, editing tags, scrobbling missed plays): click **Connect** and approve it in your browser. There is no shared secret to copy any more.
- **Your own key is still possible:** Settings › Sync › **Use my own API key instead (advanced)**. Last.fm requires Pro on the account that owns it. It is also the fallback if Last.fm ever refuses the app's key, and the message says so ("Last.fm isn't accepting the key built into Scroblytics right now").

## If you already use your own key
- A strip offers **Switch** the first time you open this version, and Settings › Sync has **Switch to Scroblytics' built-in key**. If Last.fm refuses your key, the failed sync offers **Use built-in key**. Nothing changes unless you click.
- Switching keeps your username, your history and everything else on your Mac. The only thing that is asked again is Track Actions: Last.fm's approval belongs to one key, so you click **Connect** once more.
- Your own key keeps working for as long as its owner has Last.fm Pro.

## Also new in Query
- **Album fields for tracks.** A track now has an **Album** field (the album you played it under most) that you can show as a column, sort by or filter on ("Album contains live"), plus **Album's plays** and **Album's play rank**. Together they answer questions like "the most played song from each of my top 100 albums": find Tracks where Album's play rank is at most 100, sort by Plays, and set At most 1 per album. An edition name (Deluxe, Anniversary) counts as its own album, as it does elsewhere in Query.
- **Find a field.** The lists of fields (a condition, Add sort, Show results by) open with a search box, and the Columns list has a Find a column box. Type part of a name, or a group such as "rank", and the list narrows as you type.

## Fixed
- **Fix album match › Search Apple Music Library** showed a plain album and its anniversary edition as one merged list of tracks. Each release in your Music library is now its own choice with its own tracks, so picking one no longer saves a tracklist made of two editions. Its "already played" count and the played marks also use only the plays scrobbled under that edition's own album name, so the plays of the plain album are no longer counted for its anniversary edition.

## Under the hood
- The built-in key and secret identify Scroblytics, not you, and cannot do anything on your account without your approval. They are not saved in your Keychain, so a later version can replace them.
- The diagnostic summary from Help › Report a Problem… says whether the account uses the built-in key or your own, and never shows either key.
- Help, the privacy policy and the README describe the change.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a Last.fm account. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.21.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.21.dmg.sha256`.
