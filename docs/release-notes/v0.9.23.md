Scroblytics gets ready for Last.fm's 1 December change, lets you correct how names are written, and does more to keep your data backed up.

## Last.fm's 1 December change
From 1 December 2026, Last.fm refuses most requests made with an API key whose owner doesn't have Last.fm Pro.
- **If you use the key built into Scroblytics** (the normal setup, where you only entered your username), there is nothing to do.
- **If you use your own API key without Pro,** Scroblytics now says that Pro is what's missing and, when a sync fails for that reason, offers to switch to the built-in key. Update before 1 December: earlier versions can save blank popularity figures and false "no match" album results in this situation.
- When Last.fm refuses a key (not recognised, suspended or missing Pro), background popularity and tag lookups now stop and say why, and nothing is saved as "not found".

## Correct how names are written
Fix the capitalization and spacing of artist, album and track names that Last.fm spells oddly.
- Right-click an artist, album or track and choose **Correct Name…**, or use the pencil on its page.
- **Tools › Name Corrections** finds names that look odd (different spellings, extra spaces, odd capitals and, if you choose to show them, ALL CAPS) and suggests a fix for each.
- **Corrections change only what you see in Scroblytics**. Nothing changes on Last.fm, you can reset any correction, and they are included in your backups.

## Backups
- **A reminder** under the toolbar when your last backup is over a month old, once you have created something worth keeping. You can postpone it or turn it off.
- **Automatic backup (optional, off until you turn it on):** at most once a week, and only when something changed, into a folder you choose. The newest three are kept. Set it up in **Settings › General › Backup**.
- **A safety copy of your database** is saved before the first launch of each new version. Copies are kept for two weeks (never fewer than the two newest) and listed in Settings, where you can delete them.

## Also changed
- Clicking a tab at the top now opens that section's first page, instead of the page you last left there. Back buttons and breadcrumbs work as before.

## Fixed
- **Restored album pictures didn't appear.** Restoring a backup saved your own album pictures where the app didn't look for them, and could merge the pictures of different editions. Pictures restored by an earlier version aren't repaired automatically.
- **An album's track list could seem to show one play twice** when you had played a song under two spellings. The title spelled as in the track list now takes that row, and the other spelling is listed under **Other tracks played**.
- The activity log now records Last.fm's error code when a sync fails, which helps when you report a problem.

## Requirements and install
macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a Last.fm account. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.23.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your history and everything you have created carry over. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.23.dmg.sha256`.
