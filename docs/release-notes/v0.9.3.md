# Scroblytics 0.9.3 (beta)

Less background work with very large listening histories, and an easy way to send a problem report.

## New: Help › Report a Problem…

If something goes wrong, **Help › Report a Problem…** (also in **Settings › General › Support**) gathers what is useful to send, and shows
it to you first:

- your **crash reports**, if macOS wrote any for Scroblytics, with a **Show in Finder** button;
- a short **diagnostic summary**: the versions, the kind of Mac, how many scrobbles are imported, how much memory the app is using,
  where the last sync got to, and the last events from a small local activity log (a sync starting, finishing or failing and why,
  waits for Last.fm's rate limit, Offline mode changes). It contains no Last.fm name, no keys and no music (no artist, album or track names);
- buttons to **Copy** or **Save** the summary, to start an **email** with it (and the newest crash report, if you leave that ticked), or
  to open a **GitHub issue** with it ready to paste.

Scroblytics sends nothing by itself: you press the button, and you read what is sent. After the app has quit unexpectedly, a one-line
notice under the toolbar offers the same window the next time you open it. The details are in the privacy policy and in Help › Reporting
a problem.

## Fixed: heavy CPU and memory with a very large history

A user with about 370,000 scrobbles saw the app using around 30% CPU while idle, for hours after the import. Background artwork lookups
searched the whole history once for every album, and a few screens loaded or recalculated the whole library more often than they needed to.
This version does that work once instead, and later:

- the artwork lookups use a single pass over your history instead of a search per album;
- the Ask box's personal suggestions are worked out when you first open Ask, not at launch and after every sync;
- the popularity ratings are recalculated less often on a large library.

In my testing, on a real 107,000-scrobble import the CPU after the import settled at about 5% (it was 7-14%), and on a copy of a
library enlarged to 366,000 scrobbles, opening the app took about 25 seconds of CPU (it was about 40) and peaked at about 1.0 GB of memory
(it was about 1.6 GB). A very large library is still heavier than a small one, and memory use on very large libraries is still being looked
at. If the app is slow for you, **Report a Problem…** will include its memory use.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.3.dmg` below, open it and drag
**Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with
the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.3.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.3.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.1 or 0.9.2 the app will tell you
about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
