# Scroblytics 0.9.6 (beta)

Redesigned artist, album and track pages.

## New: tighter, more useful detail pages

The pages you reach by clicking an artist, album or track are rebuilt around one card: the artwork and name, a trend line that fills the
row, the headline numbers (scrobbles or time listened, first and last scrobble, how often you play it), then the facts that belong to the
page, and the tags. Every box on the card is the same height, and a value that is still being looked up shows "Checking…" in its place,
so the page doesn't jump as things arrive.

- **Tracks** show their release date, length and tempo as tiles, each with its pencil to correct it.
- **Albums** show their release date and listen-through (tracks, turnover rate, full cycles, and how far through the current cycle you are)
  as tiles. The pencil on the **Turnover** tile opens **Fix album match…**, and an uncertain or missing match is flagged there.
- **One track table for an album.** It lists the album's whole tracklist in order, with each track's plays in the period you chose, when
  you last played it, its length, and a **Cycle** column showing whether it counts toward your current listen-through. A track you haven't
  played in the period is dimmed. Filter it to **All tracks**, **Played in period** or **Not heard this cycle**, which is the quick way to
  see what's missing on a long album. Plays that match nothing on the tracklist (a bonus or live version) are listed under their own heading.
  With no tracklist known it shows the tracks you played, as before.
- **Every sort can run either way.** An arrow beside the Sort menu reverses it (A to Z or Z to A, newest or oldest first, most or fewest
  plays), on album tracks and on an artist's albums and tracks.
- **Albums open faster.** The page used to wait for its slowest lookup (the small "#2 · 2024" rank chips) before it began the album's
  listen-through; everything now starts at once, and the track table and its filters appear in a fraction of a second.

Nothing about your data changes, and the numbers are the same ones as before: the pages read them through the same code.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.6.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.6.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.6.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
