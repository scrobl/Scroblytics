# Scroblytics 0.9.7 (beta)

Count an edition on its own, find editions that were merged by mistake, and a few smaller fixes.

## New: Correct merged albums

Scroblytics groups the edition names of an album ("Album", "Album (Deluxe Edition)", "Album (25th Anniversary Edition)") into one album, so
they share one turnover and one Last.fm match. That's right for most albums, but not when an edition has discs or tracks of its own: an
anniversary edition with demos and live recordings was measured against the standard album's tracklist, so its bonus tracks could never
count toward a listen-through and its page showed ticks beside tracks you hadn't played.

- **Fix album match…** now has a **Count this edition on its own** switch (it appears when an album has more than one edition name). Turn it
  on and that edition gets its own plays, its own Last.fm tracklist (looked up by its exact title) and its own turnover, on its page,
  Overview, the Album Turnovers tool, Missing Data and Query. The other editions stay together. Turn it off to merge it back.
- While editions are still merged, the **Turnover** tile on each page says "Counted with all editions", and its pencil opens the same sheet.
- The choice is included in your backups, like your other album matches.

- **Missing Data** has a new field, **Merged Editions**. It lists album names that are counted together with other editions of their album
although at least three of the tracks you played under that name aren't on the album's tracklist: the albums worth counting on their own.
Each row says how many tracks are off the list; click it to open Fix album match, or press **Keep Merged** if the merge is what you want and
the row won't come back. Three matching Query fields (edition names counted together, played tracks not on the tracklist, merged edition to
review) let you filter and sort the same way.

## Smaller changes

- **Overview:** clicking a cell in **Last 24 Hours** or the **Current streak** strip opens that hour or day in Query. The **Almost there**
  cards are three across and two rows deep, showing the six closest to a milestone.
- **Keychain:** Scroblytics reads your Last.fm account and tempo key once per launch instead of before every lookup, so macOS asks at most
  once if it asks at all. If the Keychain can't be read (locked, or a refused prompt), the app now says so, with Try Again, instead of
  showing the Welcome screen as if no account were saved.
- **Fixed:** a track you haven't played in the chosen period (shown dimmed on an album page) now shows its flag and heart once flagged; a
  flagged track in the Album Turnovers tool showed two flags.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.7.dmg` below, open it and drag
**Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is; the first launch adds a small table for the
editions you count on their own, and an older version may not be able to open a library a newer one has changed, so back up first (File › Back Up
Scroblytics Data…) if you may go back. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.7.dmg.sha256`
(`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.7.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the
toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
