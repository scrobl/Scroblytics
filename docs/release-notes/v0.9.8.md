# Scroblytics 0.9.8 (beta)

A much better Rabbit Hole, a fix for a long pause when switching back to the Tools tab, the artwork on the Now Playing bar, and Ask with a model running on your own Mac.

## New: a better Rabbit Hole

Rabbit Hole used to offer three tracks picked at random from Last.fm's list of similar tracks, which for many of your tracks was short or
empty, so the suggestions repeated. It now has far more to choose from and tells you why.

- **A wider pool.** Besides Last.fm's similar tracks for the current step and the two before it (so the options follow where the path has
  been), it looks at similar artists when few playable tracks come back, goes a second hop when it's still thin, and adds tracks that share
  rare tags with the current one and tracks you tend to play right after it in the same listening session.
- **Reasons.** Each suggestion shows why it's there ("Last.fm: similar to …", "you often play it after …", "shares tags …"), and a "Why these
  three" list shows more. It also says how many options remain.
- **Steering.** **Lean toward** (forgotten or familiar), **Variety**, and **Avoid repeats** (it remembers what it showed for a week), under a
  collapsed **Style** row; the per-artist and per-album limits and your conditions sit under **Filters**. New Suggestions reuses the cached
  pool, so it's instant.
- **Start from…** beside Pick Starting Track: what's playing now, a forgotten favourite, one of your top tracks, or a surprise, and it makes
  sure the first step isn't empty.
- **Take Steps for Me…** extends the path for 30, 60 or 90 minutes (or 10, 20 or 30 tracks) following your Style, backing out of dead ends,
  and stopping at once if you press Stop. Every step is an ordinary step in the tree, so Back One Step and **Export to Apple Music
  playlist** work on a walk, and **Remove Last Walk** takes it off again.
- The page stays scrolled to the bottom after each step, the artwork no longer flashes when a suggestion becomes a step

Last.fm's answers are kept on your Mac for 30 days (only successful lookups), in a small new table that the first launch adds. Rabbit Hole
still only ever suggests tracks you have already played.

## Fixed: switching back to a tab no longer freezes the window

With an artist, album or track page open inside **Tools** (for example after following a path in Rabbit Hole), switching to the tab from
another one could freeze the window for many seconds. Every page in that stack blanked itself and loaded everything again, and several of
those loads ran on the main thread. On a library of this size the window stayed unresponsive for most of 20 seconds in testing.

- **A page you return to keeps what it had.** Artist, album and track pages no longer blank and reload when you switch back to their tab or
  step back to them; they reload as before when you change the date range, sync, or correct something, and after ten minutes away.
- **The page's picture is looked up on its own.** Each page used to rebuild an index of every picture in your library (about a third of a
  second each time) just to find one; it now reads only the few rows it needs, in under a millisecond.
- **Rabbit Hole reads the library once.** It re-read everything each time its tab appeared, on the main thread. It now reads it in the
  background and only again if it's been five minutes.
- **Large libraries.** On a library of 360,000 scrobbles in a macOS 15 test machine, the artist and track pages worked out their
  "#2 · 2024" rank chips on the main thread and kept the window frozen for 10 seconds or more each time; they now do it in the background
  (the same answers, checked against the old way on real data). Multi-Album Tracks and Rabbit Hole also read the library in a way that put
  the whole read on the main thread, which is fixed. In the same walk through every tab and several tools, the longest freeze went from
  12 seconds to 2.
- **The same fix for other tools.** Missing Data, Similar Sounds, Library Gaps, Tag Management and Ask's suggestions all read the library's
  background data in a way that could freeze the window while they loaded; they now do it off the main thread.

## Fixed: Now Playing shows the same artwork as the rest of the app

The Now Playing bar used the cover Last.fm reported for the play, which can be another edition's, so a song could show different
artwork there than on its album and track pages. It now shows the artwork those pages show (your own image, a saved or inherited album
image) and uses Last.fm's only when there is nothing else.

## Fixed: Ask works much better with Ollama and other small local models

Asked "my most played song by The Cure", a local model could come back with a query that ignored the artist and capped the results at
one per artist, per album and per year, and "top 10 songs by …" lost its artist filter and sort altogether. Ask now:

- **Ignores filled-in blanks.** A local model told to write every key fills the ones it doesn't need with zeros and made-up dates
  ("compare with 0 days earlier", a date range on a name). Those are ignored instead of failing the whole condition.
- **Keeps a diversity cap only when you asked for one** ("per artist", "each", "different artists"…), not when a model adds it on its own.
- **Adds the artist filter for "by <an artist in your library>"** when the model left it out, and lists it in "Here's how I understood that".
- **Applies a count you stated** ("top 50", "10 most recently played", "find 25 songs similar to …", "my most played song") when the model
  left it out.
- **Asks Ollama for a leaner answer.** Only the parts a query needs are requested, so answers take a couple of seconds instead of 10–20,
  and there is less for a small model to get wrong. On Scroblytics's own set of 66 test questions, a 14-billion-parameter model on a
  Mac went from missing the first ten (stopped there) to getting 58 of 66 right, at about 1.6 seconds each.

The first question after a long pause is still slow while Ollama loads the model and reads Ask's instructions, so Ask now says so under
the text box (and in Settings › Ask) for Ollama and other servers on your own Mac, along with how a 14-billion-parameter model measured.

Nothing about your data changes.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.8.dmg` below, open it and drag
**Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is; the first launch adds a small table, and an older version may not be able to open a library a newer one has changed, so back up first (File › Back Up Scroblytics Data…) if you may go back. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with
the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.8.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.8.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you
about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
