# Scroblytics 0.9.9 (beta)

A **Share** button across the app that turns what you're looking at into an image or text for friends or a Reddit post, a choice of Last.fm logo, and two fixes.

## New: Share

A Share button now sits where something worth showing is. It opens a preview of a card made from exactly what's on screen, with the options that suit that card, and four ways to send it on: **Copy Image** (paste it into a post or message), **Save PNG…**, **Share…** (the macOS Share menu: Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Notes…) and **Copy Text** (a Markdown table, which Reddit shows as a table, a Markdown list, or plain text). Cards are square, portrait or wide, in one dark design, and are made on your Mac: nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it or pick a target.

- **Top Charts:** the chart as ranked, with movement against the comparison and "Movement since …" saying what it's measured against.
- **Artist, album and track pages:** a card with the page's numbers, yearly rank and milestone badges, and the trend line, for the period the page is showing.
- **Milestones:** a list of the milestones you've reached for the category and tier on screen, with the coloured tier pills, and a share icon on each reached milestone for a card of that one. The library's own total milestones can show a trophy or the Last.fm logo.
- **Timeline:** each month has a Share button for a recap: its totals, top artist, album and track, and the milestones reached.
- **Overview:** a **Share** button at the top right makes a recap of the period shown: scrobbles (or time listened), artists, albums and tracks, the change against your comparison (for All time, which has nothing to compare with, it says when you started scrobbling), new artists, scrobbles per day, your streak, and your top artist, album and track.
- **Query and Ask:** the results as listed, showing one column of your choice (or none) with every column in the text copy. For a question put to Ask, the question is the title.
- **Rabbit Hole:** the path as a list (with why each track was chosen) or as a tree showing what was on offer at each step.
- **Trends:** each chart has a Share button that makes a card of the chart as it's drawn, with its title, period, and (where there is one) the headline number, the comparison and the legend.
- **Collage Generator:** **Copy Image** and **Share…** beside Save PNG, and optional lines under the collage for what and when, your Last.fm name and "Scroblytics".

By default a card says nothing about you: no Last.fm name and no "Scroblytics" credit. Turn either on in the preview or in **Settings › General › Sharing**; the choice is remembered. You can also type your own title on a list card, and every card has a switch for today's date.

## New: choose the Last.fm logo

**Settings › General › Sharing** has a **Last.fm logo** choice: the Last.fm logo (the default), its "last.fm" wordmark, a simple badge, or none. It goes in front of your name on shared cards and on the Now Playing bar, which used to show a plain grey badge.

## Fixed: Ask and "Top Album by Release Year"

Asked for the "top album by release year", Ask could list the most played albums overall instead of the best one from each year. A singular "top / best / most played" album, track or song followed by "by release year" now means one per release year. The plural ("top albums by release year") is left to the model, since it can just mean sorted by year.

## Fixed: three-digit ranks wrapping

In an artist's Tracks list (Library › an artist with hundreds of tracks) and in an album's track table, a rank of 100 or more wrapped onto two lines. The column is now wide enough for four digits.

## Under the hood

Cards are drawn from what each screen already holds, so they match what you see and cost no extra lookups. A very long list is cut to the rows a card can show, a picture that won't download is given up on after eight seconds, and rendering was checked on a library of 367,000 scrobbles. Nothing about your data changes, and the update adds no new table.

## Requirements and install

macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon or Intel) and a free Last.fm API key, as before. Download `Scroblytics-0.9.9.dmg` below, open it and drag **Scroblytics** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your data stays where it is. To verify the download, compare its SHA-256 with the attached `Scroblytics-0.9.9.dmg.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 Scroblytics-0.9.9.dmg`). If you're on 0.9.3 or earlier the app will tell you about this version (a strip under the toolbar, or **Scroblytics › Check for Updates…**).
